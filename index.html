Personal · live demo

Drone Tracking

Detect and track vehicles and pedestrians in drone footage, where 60% of objects are smaller than 32×32 pixels. Each object keeps its identity from frame to frame. The detector is YOLO fine-tuned on VisDrone; the tiling, the multi-object tracker (Kalman filter + Hungarian matching, ByteTrack-style) and the mAP / MOTA / IDF1 metrics are written from scratch and checked against the official tools.

  • 0.144 → 0.343 mAP@50 after 50 epochs on one L4 GPU
  • 0.169 → 0.281 mAP@50 on small objects with tiling, and why SAHI's default merging made it worse
  • MOTA 0.193, IDF1 0.416 vs 0.177 / 0.403 for Ultralytics ByteTrack on the same detections

Personal

Karaokit

Turn any song into a karaoke video. Vocals are removed, the lyrics are timed word by word, and each word lights up as it's sung, including right-to-left languages. Runs locally in Docker with a web UI, or offloads the heavy models to a cloud GPU.

  • BS-Roformer source separation for vocals / instrumental
  • CTC forced alignment (Meta MMS) for word-level timing
  • ~1 min per song on GPU, with a library of everything you've made
